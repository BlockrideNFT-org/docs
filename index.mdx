## What is Blockride?
Blockride is a Solana-based DeFi platform that structures fractionalized, asset-backed, on-chain vehicle financing to enable transparent capital formation and fleet expansion across Africa, addressing two key participants in the transportation business:
<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Investors" icon="users">
    Individuals or entities interested in investing in the transportation sector.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Fleet Operators" icon="user">
    Licensed operators seeking financing to purchase buses for commercial transportation.
  </Card>
</Columns>

## Why This Matters
Africa faces a structural financing gap in mass mobility. Limited access to affordable vehicle financing constrains fleet expansion, contributes to fragmented transport systems, increases private motorization, and exacerbates congestion and carbon emissions.

By mobilizing community capital into structured, asset-backed vehicle financing, Blockride:
- Expands access to mass mobility infrastructure
- Reduces reliance on informal and inefficient transport systems
- Lowers congestion and time spent in traffic
- Supports reductions in carbon emissions
- Enables more affordable and accessible public transportation
- Promotes financial inclusion by allowing broader participation in vehicle financing opportunities
