## What is Blockride?
Blockride is a mobility fintech platform focused on advancing mass transit by structuring revenue-based financing that enables licensed fleet operators to acquire buses, with investors repaid over time from operating income.

This solution addresses two key participants in the transportation business sector:

  <Card title="Investors" icon="users">

  </Card>
  <Card title="Fleet Operators" icon="user">
    Licensed operators seeking financing to purchase buses for commercial transportation.
  </Card>
</Columns>
## Why This Matters
When people cannot move efficiently, entire economies slow down. Access to jobs, education, and healthcare depends on reliable transportation. When transit fails, opportunity shrinks.

[*Enrique Peñalosa*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enrique_Pe%C3%B1alosa), credited with transforming Bogotá into a model of equitable public transit, put it simply:
> *A developed country is not one where the poor own cars, but one where the rich use public transportation.*

Africa's cities are growing fast. The demand for reliable, high-capacity transit is urgent and accelerating. What is missing is not the need, but the capital to meet it.

That is the problem Blockride was built to solve.
