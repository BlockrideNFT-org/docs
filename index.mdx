## What is Blockride?
Blockride is a mobility fintech platform focused on advancing mass transit by structuring revenue-based financing that enables licensed fleet operators to acquire buses, with investors repaid over time from operating income.

This solution addresses two key participants in the transportation business sector:

## Why This Matters
A well-functioning transportation system is essential for national development. Citizens must be able to move efficiently, safely, and affordably to access jobs, education, healthcare, and commerce. When public transit is inaccessible, unreliable, or costly, economic participation suffers, productivity declines, and urban congestion worsens.

This principle reflects the broader truth about development and mobility. As [Enrique Peñalosa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enrique_Pe%C3%B1alosa) famously stated:

> "A developed country is not one where the poor own cars, but one where the rich use public transportation.”

In Africa, limited vehicle financing further compounds these challenges. Fleet operators often face high interest rates, opaque processes, and mandatory down payments, which restrict their ability to expand or modernize their buses.

Addressing mass mobility challenges through vehicle financing solutions like Blockride enables fleet operators to expand operations, improving mobility, economic efficiency, and sustainable development.
